# Product

## Register

product

## Users
- **Teller** at the Skyline Exchange counter (Jl. Bypass Ngurah Rai, Kuta, Bali). Uses the POS all day on a desktop at the counter, sometimes on a phone or tablet. Job: record buy/sell foreign-currency transactions fast and correctly, print the thermal receipt, handle delivery orders.
- **Owner / supervisor**. Opens the Settings area occasionally: Google Sheets backup, store profile and receipt, cashier accounts, database backup, Bank Indonesia compliance (KUPVA BB). Job: configure once, then trust it.

## Product Purpose
Point-of-sale for a licensed money changer (KUPVA BB). It must make every transaction fast, accurate and auditable: correct rates, clean receipts, a cloud copy in Google Sheets, and BI compliance checks (USD 10K threshold, PEP EDD, DTTOT screening). Success = no wrong totals, no lost records, no compliance gaps, no waiting customers.

## Brand Personality
Trustworthy, fast, transparent. Fintech-grade calm: the interface states facts plainly, shows true status (never a decorative "active" badge), and stays out of the teller's way.

## Anti-references
- Generic SaaS templates: identical icon cards, gradients, glassmorphism, decorative big-number stat tiles.
- Small, dim text: 10–11px muted gray labels that are hard to read on a bright counter screen.

## Design Principles
1. **Status is truth.** Any badge or color states a real condition (connected, not configured, failed). Never decoration.
2. **One primary action per area.** Secondary actions recede; the thing to do next is obvious.
3. **Readable at a glance.** Counter lighting and quick glances: favor size and contrast over density.
4. **Settings are configured once.** Group by task, explain only what is needed, confirm saves clearly.
5. **Money is precise.** Numbers tabular, units explicit, nothing ambiguous.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
WCAG 2.1 AA: text contrast ≥ 4.5:1, visible keyboard focus, touch targets ≥ 44px on tablet/phone, respect `prefers-reduced-motion`.
